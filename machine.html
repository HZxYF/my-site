Machine

Two things that do something when you touch them: a box that refuses to stay on, and a game that times your hand.

The useless machine

A switch, a box, and an arm inside it. Turn the switch on and the arm comes out and turns it off again. It has no other function. This is the whole point.

The machine is off. It would like to stay that way.

The game: how fast are you?

Five tries. Press start, wait for the pad to turn green, then click as fast as you can. Clicking early costs you the try.

Five tries. Wait for green, then click.
